This policy explains what the Clipora website collects and how it is used. Clipora is a product of Esar.App, developed by Ekeme Rejoice. The Clipora app will have its own policy when it launches.

What we collect

The only information you give us on this website is the email address you type into the waitlist form. We do not ask for your name, phone number or payment details.

How we use it

  • To tell you when Clipora early access opens.
  • To send occasional updates about Clipora. You can ask us to stop at any time.

We do not sell your email address.

Where it is stored

Waitlist emails are stored with the service we connect to the form, such as Firebase. That provider keeps the data on our behalf.

Other services this site loads

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Cloudinary. When your browser loads them, those services can see your IP address and basic browser details. Our hosting provider may also keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete your email address at any time. Send a message to your-email@example.com and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
